Stroop Challenge

Your brain reads words faster than it names colors. Override that reflex: answer by the rule shown, before the ring runs out.

How to play

  • Ink Color: press the color the word is printed in, ignoring what it says.
  • Word Text: press the color the word names, ignoring its ink.
  • Standard gives 2.8 s per word for 10 points; Accelerated gives 1.5 s, always mismatches word and ink, and scores 15.
  • Neuro-Plasticity blends your accuracy (60%) and speed (40%).
